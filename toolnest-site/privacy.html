Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This is a starter privacy notice for the initial ToolNest website. Review and adapt it to your actual hosting, analytics, advertising and contact-form setup before public launch. It is not legal advice.

Information processed by tools

The image compressor, word counter, GPA calculator and JSON formatter are designed to process the information you enter locally in your browser. ToolNest does not intentionally upload that tool input to its own application server. The QR generator loads a JavaScript library from a third-party content delivery network; the text you enter is processed in your browser by that library.

Hosting and server logs

Our hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, referrer and request time to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the website. Check your hosting provider's current privacy documentation for details.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The initial version does not intentionally set first-party advertising cookies. If analytics, Google AdSense or other advertising services are added, update this policy to identify the providers, explain their data and cookie practices, and provide any consent or opt-out controls required in the countries where visitors live. Third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own policies.

Third-party services

External services such as font delivery, QR code library hosting, website hosting, analytics and advertising may receive technical information when your browser requests their resources. Review and list the services actually enabled on the live website.

Children

ToolNest is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact the site operator.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice when the website's features or service providers change. Replace the placeholder contact address on the Contact page with a monitored email address before launch.
