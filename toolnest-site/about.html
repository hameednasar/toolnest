OUR PURPOSE

About ToolNest

ToolNest is a growing collection of straightforward online utilities designed to help people finish everyday digital tasks with less friction.

Our approach

We aim to make each tool easy to understand, accessible on common devices and useful without requiring an account. Where practical, tools process information locally in your browser instead of sending it to our servers.

What we're building

Our initial collection includes an image compressor, word counter, GPA calculator, JSON formatter and QR code generator. We plan to improve these tools and add new ones based on real user needs.

Questions or suggestions?

We welcome feedback and ideas. Visit our contact page to get in touch.
