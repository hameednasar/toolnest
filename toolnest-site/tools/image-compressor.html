Image Compressor

Make image files smaller in seconds. Your image is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to our server.

Tip: WebP often produces smaller files. PNG is useful when you need transparency. Compression results vary by image and format.

How to compress an image

  1. Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image from your device.
  2. Adjust the quality slider and select an output format.
  3. Select Compress image, then download the result.

Is my image uploaded?

No. The image is read and processed by your browser. It is not sent to ToolNest's server. Avoid using a shared device if your image is private.

Which format should I choose?

WebP is a good general-purpose choice for smaller files. JPEG works well for photographs. PNG is useful for graphics that need lossless output or transparency.
