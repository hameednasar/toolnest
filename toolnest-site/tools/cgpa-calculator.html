How is GPA calculated?

GPA is calculated as the sum of each course's credit hours multiplied by its grade points, divided by the total credit hours.

GPA = Σ (credit hours × grade points) ÷ Σ credit hours

Example

If you earn 4.0 in a 3-credit course and 3.0 in a 3-credit course, your GPA is (3 × 4.0 + 3 × 3.0) ÷ 6 = 3.50.

Does every university use the same scale?

No. Grade-point scales and rounding rules vary. The dropdown uses a common 4.0-style scale as a starting point, not an official scale for every institution.
