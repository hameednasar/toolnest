How the word counter works

Paste or type text into the box. ToolNest updates the word, character and sentence counts immediately. The reading-time estimate uses an approximate reading speed of 200 words per minute.

What counts as a word?

Words are separated by whitespace. This gives a practical estimate for most everyday writing, though different writing systems and punctuation conventions may count words differently.

Is my text stored?

No. Counting happens in your browser. Your text is not uploaded to our server.
